How to Play

  1. 1
    Start a new game: A secret word is randomly picked by the backend.
  2. 2
    Guess one letter at a time: Click the on-screen letters or press keyboard keys.
  3. 3
    Correct guesses reveal letters: Positions will be displayed on the word board.
  4. 4
    Incorrect guesses reduce chances: Each wrong guess draws a piece of the hangman.
  5. 5
    Win before 6 mistakes: Guess the entire word before reaching 6 incorrect attempts!
⚖️

Game Rules

  • ✓ Maximum 6 Incorrect Guesses: On the 6th wrong guess, the game is over.
  • ✓ No Repeated Penalty: Guessing the same letter twice is prevented and will not cost you an attempt.
  • ✓ Case-Insensitive: Both lowercase and uppercase letters are accepted identically.
  • ✓ Scoring System:
    Win = 100 - (10 × Incorrect Guesses)

    Example: Win with 2 mistakes = 80 points. Losses award 0 points.

  • ✓ Predefined Dictionary: Features 5 core tech words: PYTHON, DJANGO, COMPUTER, PROGRAM, DATABASE.
